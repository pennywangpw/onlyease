About Only Ease

Our Story

Three generations, 40 years of wellness expertise. OnlyEase combines research, innovation, and strict quality control to create natural and effective health products. From pineapple enzymes and probiotics to enzyme soaps and skincare, we turn wellness into everyday living.

The Beginning

We started in the lab with one goal: creating safe and effective wellness products. After years of research and development, we introduced pineapple enzymes, nattokinase red yeast rice, probiotics, Antrodia cinnamomea, cordyceps, and more. Every formula follows the principles of balanced ingredients and effective dosage.

Today

OnlyEase is redefining healthy drinks. Our sugar-free enzyme tea drinks replace artificial sugars with natural pineapple enzymes, creating “drinkable skincare” that supports daily wellness while tasting refreshing and natural.

The Future

OnlyEase is growing from an enzyme drink brand into a complete healthy lifestyle brand. We offer wellness drinks, enzyme soaps, probiotics, and personalized nutrition guidance to help people make health a simple part of daily life.

Our Vision

We believe wellness should be natural, simple, and enjoyable. By developing additive-free products and personalized wellness solutions, we hope to help everyone live healthier, happier, and more balanced lives.
