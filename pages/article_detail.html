What Are Enzymes and Why Are They Important?

Enzymes are natural proteins that help speed up essential chemical reactions in the body. One of their most important roles is supporting digestion by helping break down carbohydrates, proteins, and fats into smaller nutrients that the body can absorb and use for energy.

Digestive enzymes are naturally produced in the mouth, stomach, pancreas, and intestines. Common enzymes include:

  • Amylase — helps digest carbohydrates
  • Protease — helps break down proteins
  • Lipase — helps digest fats

Enzymes may help support:

  • Healthy digestion
  • Nutrient absorption
  • Reduced bloating and digestive discomfort

Certain enzyme-rich foods such as pineapple, papaya, fermented foods, and kefir naturally contain enzymes that may support digestive health. Pineapple contains bromelain, while papaya contains papain — both known for helping break down protein.
