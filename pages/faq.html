What is an enzyme drink?

An enzyme drink is a fermented beverage made from fruits, vegetables, or plants. During fermentation, natural enzymes, amino acids, and organic compounds are produced, helping support digestion, metabolism, and overall wellness. OnlyEase enzyme drinks use naturally fermented pineapple enzymes with no added sugar or artificial ingredients.

Why does the color of pineapple enzyme drink vary?

The color may naturally vary from batch to batch due to the fermentation process. During fermentation, microorganisms break down sugars, proteins, and fibers in the fruit, producing amino acids and organic acids that can affect both color and flavor.

Oxidation, fermentation time, sugar content, and temperature may also influence the color. Longer fermentation can create a natural browning effect called the Maillard reaction, which may turn the liquid amber, tea-colored, or darker.

Can children and pregnant women consume pineapple enzyme drinks?

Children over 1 year old may consume enzyme drinks in moderate amounts. However, if there are no specific digestive or health concerns, enzyme supplements may not be necessary for young children. Pregnant women should always be cautious with dietary supplements and functional foods. It is recommended to consult a healthcare professional and wait until the pregnancy is stable before consuming enzyme products.

Can pineapple enzyme drinks be mixed with hot water?

No. Enzymes are sensitive to high temperatures, which can reduce their activity. It is recommended to use water below 40°C (104°F) when mixing or diluting the drink.
