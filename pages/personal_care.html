Enzyme Soap by OnlyEase is a functional cleansing soap designed with natural fruit enzymes and botanical extracts. Inspired by the concept of “skincare through cleansing,” it helps gently remove excess oil, dirt, and impurities while maintaining the skin’s natural moisture balance.

Unlike traditional soaps that may feel drying, enzyme soap offers a mild and skin-friendly cleansing experience suitable for face, body, and even light makeup removal. Enriched with plant-based ingredients, it leaves the skin feeling soft, smooth, and refreshed after every wash.

With a variety of natural scents and formulations such as avocado, citrus, berry, rose, marine algae, and mushroom, each enzyme soap is designed to deliver a unique cleansing experience while supporting healthy, radiant-looking skin.
